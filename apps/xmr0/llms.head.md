# xmr0.uk — Monero privacy guides: how a Monero payment stays private

> Independent guides to Monero privacy: how ring signatures, stealth addresses and confidential amounts hide who paid, who was paid and how much, and where that protection ends. Written in British English. Every figure names where it came from.

How this site works, in three rules:

- **Every figure names where it came from.** A parameter, a date or a version number is the Monero project's, its code's or the publisher's, and the guide says which and when it was checked.
- **No price talk, no trading advice.** The guides are about how the privacy works, not about buying or selling.
- **Nothing here is paid for.** No project, exchange or company pays to be mentioned, ranked or linked.

## What a reader finds here

- [Guides](https://xmr0.uk/guides/): every Monero privacy guide, newest first.
- [About](https://xmr0.uk/about/): the rules above, written out at length.
- [Everything in one file](https://xmr0.uk/llms-full.txt): every page as Markdown. Each page is also at its own address with `.md` appended.

## Where the figures come from

The Monero project's own documentation, research papers and source code, and the organisations that publish the figures a guide cites. Each guide names its source and the date it was checked.
