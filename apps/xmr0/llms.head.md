# xmr0.uk — Monero privacy, explained: the protocol, the network and the wallet

> Independent guides to Monero privacy for readers who know security: how ring signatures, one-time addresses and RingCT hide who paid, who was paid and how much; how Dandelion++, Tor and I2P keep a node's address out of a transaction; what view keys, proofs and seeds disclose; and where each protection ends. Written in British English. Every figure names the code, specification or paper it came from and the date it was checked.

How this site works, in four rules:

- **Every figure names where it came from.** A parameter, a date or a version number is the Monero project's, its code's, a specification's or a paper's, and the guide says which and when it was checked.
- **No price talk, no trading advice.** The guides are about how the privacy works, not about buying or selling.
- **Privacy is a security property.** It is explained like encryption: a mechanism, a threat model and limits, and how a holder can disclose a payment on purpose.
- **Nothing here is paid for.** No project, exchange or company pays to be mentioned, ranked or linked.

## What a reader finds here

- [Guides](https://xmr0.uk/guides/): every Monero privacy guide, newest first.
- [The protocol](https://xmr0.uk/protocol/): ring signatures, one-time addresses, RingCT, FCMP++ and the comparison with Zcash.
- [The network](https://xmr0.uk/network/): Dandelion++, and running monerod over Tor or I2P.
- [The wallet](https://xmr0.uk/wallets/): view keys and payment proofs, seeds and Polyseed, signed downloads and hardware wallets.
- [About](https://xmr0.uk/about/): what the site covers and the rules above, written out at length.
- [Everything in one file](https://xmr0.uk/llms-full.txt): every page as Markdown. Each page is also at its own address with `.md` appended.

Mining is not covered here: Monero's proof of work, RandomX, is the subject of [random.directory](https://random.directory/).

## Where the figures come from

The Monero source code (monero-project/monero) and its documentation, Moneropedia, the FCMP++ stressnet release notes, the Carrot and Polyseed specifications, the Dandelion++ paper, the Zcash Improvement Proposals, EUR-Lex for EU law and the Financial Conduct Authority for the UK. Each guide names its source and the date it was checked.
