# zerocake.org — Monzero in Cake Wallet: the XMZ integration, explained

> Guides to the patch that adds Monzero (XMZ), a fork of Monero with a chain of its own, to Cake Wallet: what it adds, BIP39 seeds, nodes, how it was built and tested, and where its pull request stands. Written in English by the people who prepared the patch; not run or endorsed by Cake Wallet or the Monzero project.

How this site works, in four rules:

- **Every figure names where it came from.** Facts about the patch come from the patch and its pull request text; facts about Monzero from its own specification and README. Each guide says which, and when it was checked.
- **Our builds and tests are marked as ours.** A build, a test or a node query says it was ours, with the machine and the toolchain, and what was not tested is listed too.
- **Nothing to download.** The site describes the patch and the build; it does not distribute them.
- **No price talk, and nothing paid for.** No project, exchange or company pays to be mentioned or linked.

## Where the figures come from

The Monzero patch series for Cake Wallet and its pull request text; Monzero's chain specification and README on GitHub; Cake Wallet's and monero_c's public repositories; and our own build, tests and queries of node.monzero.org, which say they are ours. Each guide names its source and the date it was checked.
