# London Calendar — London's big dates for the year ahead

> The fixed points of the London year, published as soon as their organisers fix the dates. Every entry names the source it came from and the day it was checked against it. Published by GB7 (gb7.uk). Independent: not a government outlet, not a ticket seller, not affiliated with any venue.

How this site works, in five rules:

- **Every fact names where it came from, and when we last looked.** The source prints as text, never as a live link.
- **Nothing is listed on a date we could not confirm.** A date that has only been reported, and not published by the organiser, waits.
- **What's on, not what went wrong.** No politics, no crime, no tragedy.
- **We pick the best, and say why.** Picking out the best of something is the job; running a named business down is not.
- **A page exists when there is something to put on it.** No empty templates waiting for content.

## What a reader finds here

- [Events](https://l0n.uk/events/): the year's big London dates, in date order, with times, prices and the ticket release where the organiser published them.
- [News](https://l0n.uk/news/): dates as they are fixed and tickets as they go on sale.
- [About](https://l0n.uk/about/): the rules above, written out at length.

## Where the facts come from

The organisers themselves: the Mayor of London, the Boat Race, London Marathon Events, the Royal Horticultural Society and the rest. Each entry prints that name and the date it was verified. What has not been announced is said to be unannounced.
