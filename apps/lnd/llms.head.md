# lnd.lol — what's on in the outer London boroughs

> Listings and news for the outer London boroughs — Hackney, Wandsworth, Sutton, Southwark, Newham and their neighbours, not central London. Every entry names the source it came from and the day it was checked against it. Independent publication: not a government outlet, not a ticket seller, not affiliated with any venue.

How this site works, in five rules:

- **Every fact names where it came from, and when we last looked.** The source prints as text, never as a live link.
- **Nothing is listed on a date we could not confirm.** If the organiser said "early November" and nothing more, no invented day appears here — the missing day appears instead.
- **What's on, not what went wrong.** No politics, no crime, no tragedy.
- **We pick the best, and say why.** Picking out the best of something is the job; running a named business down is not.
- **A page exists when there is something to put on it.** No empty templates waiting for content.

## What a reader finds here

- [Events](https://lnd.lol/events/): events on dates confirmed with the organiser, with times, prices and running order where the organiser published them.
- [News](https://lnd.lol/news/): what changed in the city. Long pieces carrying the whole calendar or the whole figure, not a wire paragraph.
- [About](https://lnd.lol/about/): the rules above, written out at length.

## Where the facts come from

The borough press, or the organiser: Hackney Citizen, Wandsworth Guardian, East London Advertiser, Sutton Guardian, Southwark News, This Is Local London. Each entry prints that name and the date it was verified. Venue coordinates are geocoded once and stored in the data; where the geocoder returns the wrong place, the entry publishes without a map rather than with a wrong one.

Where a voice from Reddit appears in the text, it is a direct quotation from a public post, attributed to the subreddit rather than to the person.

What has not been announced is said to be unannounced. A closing date nobody has given, a theme not yet confirmed and a running order still to come are three things this site names rather than fills in.
