# sol2go.lol — where Solana meets in person

> A calendar and a newsfeed for the Solana ecosystem: hackathons, conferences and city meetups, plus the wider crypto calendar they sit in. Every entry names the source it came from and the day it was checked against it. Independent publication: not affiliated with the Solana Foundation, not an exchange, not a ticket seller, and not a financial adviser.

How this site works, in five rules:

- **Every fact names where it came from, and when we last looked.** The source prints as text, never as a live link.
- **Nothing is listed on a date we could not confirm.** A date given only as a month is printed as a month.
- **Events and what people build, not prices.** Where a story turns on a market number, that number carries a plain warning that it is not advice.
- **Nothing here is financial advice.** No token is recommended, promoted or rated, and no listing is paid for.
- **A page exists when there is something to put on it.** No empty templates waiting for content.

## What a reader finds here

- [Events](https://sol2go.lol/events/): hackathons, conferences and meetups on dates confirmed with the organiser, with the venue, the format and the registration route where the organiser published them.
- [News](https://sol2go.lol/news/): releases, tooling and programmes — the things that change what a builder can do this month.
- [About](https://sol2go.lol/about/): the rules above, written out at length.

## Where the facts come from

The organiser, or the publication that reported it: the Solana Foundation and solana.com, Colosseum, ETHGlobal, the conference organisers themselves, and the project repositories and release notes. Each entry prints that name and the date it was verified.

What has not been announced is said to be unannounced. A venue nobody has given, an agenda not yet published and a prize pool still to come are three things this site names rather than fills in.
