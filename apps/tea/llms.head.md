# tea.ceo — tea guides, brewing times and the tea calendar

> An independent guide to brewing tea, kind by kind and tea by tea — water temperature, steeping time and how much leaf — and a calendar of tea times and tea days with a live countdown to each. Written in British English. Every figure names where it came from.

How this site works, in four rules:

- **Every figure names where it came from.** A temperature or a steeping time is the grower's, the tea house's or the standard's, and the guide says whose. Where respectable sources disagree, the guide gives both.
- **A story is told as a story.** Where a tale about tea cannot be checked — the duchess, the mandarin — the page says it is the tale and not the record.
- **Nothing here is paid for.** No brand or tea house pays to be mentioned, ranked or linked.
- **Your clock, not ours.** Tea times count down on the reader's own clock, and reminders are calendar files the reader keeps.

## What a reader finds here

- [Guides](https://tea.ceo/guides/): how to brew black, green, oolong, white and pu-erh tea, and named teas within them — Darjeeling, Earl Grey, sencha, matcha, Dragon Well.
- [Tea times](https://tea.ceo/schedule/): early morning tea, breakfast tea, elevenses, afternoon tea, high tea and the bedtime cuppa on the reader's own clock, and the tea days of the year, each with a calendar file at `/schedule/<slug>.ics` and all of them at `/tea-times.ics`.
- One page per family of tea once it has two guides: [black tea](https://tea.ceo/black-tea/) and [green tea](https://tea.ceo/green-tea/).
- [About](https://tea.ceo/about/): the rules above, written out at length.
- [Everything in one file](https://tea.ceo/llms-full.txt): every page as Markdown. Each page is also at its own address with `.md` appended.

## Where the figures come from

Growers and tea houses that publish their own methods (Ippodo for matcha and sencha, among others), the ISO 3103 tasting standard, the Royal Society of Chemistry's 2003 method, the Tea Board of India for what may be called Darjeeling, and the United Nations for International Tea Day. Each guide names its source and the date it was checked.
